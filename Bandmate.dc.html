Anyone can ship IELTS questions. Almost nobody can ship a tutor who hears you stall at 0:41, interrupts, and drills that exact habit tomorrow.

Streaks and XP exist but never lead. They sit as small type in a corner; the band forecast is the number that gets the big display size. Nobody pays £15/month to feed an owl — they pay to pass.

Mira picks length and content daily, so the home screen shows one session card with a stated duration and a stated reason. No unit map, no level select — the plan is her job, not the learner's.
